Clinical Shift Scratchpad

A quick list for ward jobs during a shift. It stays on your device and clears itself when you’re done.

It’s a phone app, so there’s no web version to open.

Source code and notes on GitHub

Before you use it

Don’t put anything that could identify a patient in the app, in screenshots or in the code.

Your jobs stay on your phone. There’s no account, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Jobs expire after 24 hours unless you change the setting. They’re removed when the app next checks the list. If you copy the end-of-shift summary, other apps on your phone can read it.

What it won’t do

This is a personal prototype I’ve used on my own shifts, not finished software. It hasn’t been through the formal safety, regulatory and data-protection checks a clinical product would need, and it doesn’t keep a record of changes.
